About Me

My name is Hyunsu, I am a Nuke Compositor with 5+ years of experience in feature and episodic VFX, focused on CG/live-action integration, shot finaling, and sequence consistency.

Alongside compositing, I have a technical background in Python, BlinkScript, and Nuke tool development. I use these skills to create artist-friendly workflows, reusable comp setups, and practical tools that reduce repetitive work and support more consistent production compositing.
